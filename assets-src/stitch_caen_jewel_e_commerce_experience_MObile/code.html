Questions, Answered

Essential details regarding acquisition, diamond certification, bespoke sizing, and aftercare.

Every CAEN JEWEL diamond above 0.30 carat arrives with dual certification cards from IGI (International Gemological Institute) or GIA (Gemological Institute of America). Each diamond bears a microscopic laser inscription on its girdle matching the physical certificate.
Orders are dispatched via specialized armored couriers (BVC or Sequel Secure) in tamper-evident, unmarked discreet packaging. Shipments are 100% insured up to point of OTP-verified physical handover. Delivery takes 3–5 business days across India.
