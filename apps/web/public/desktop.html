CLIENT ASSISTANCE

Questions, Answered

Essential details regarding acquisition, diamond certification, bespoke sizing, and aftercare.

Ready-to-ship archival pieces are dispatched within 48 business hours via insured overnight transit. Bespoke custom ring sizing and made-to-order gold creations are sculpted and hallmarked within 7 to 10 working days prior to secure vault courier delivery.
Every single Caen diamond above 0.30 carats arrives accompanied by individual laboratory certification from the International Gemological Institute (IGI) or Gemological Institute of America (GIA), paired with our Atelier Certificate of Authenticity.
We provide a discreet 15-day inspection window from the moment of signature confirmation. If a non-custom piece does not entirely fulfill your expectations, we arrange fully insured reverse vault courier collection for an exchange or prompt refund.
Our atelier welcomes custom commissions. You may request alternate gold carats (18K yellow, rose, or platinum), select specific diamond grades, or commission an original heirloom design with our master artisans through private salon consultation.
Avoid wearing fine gold jewellery while immersed in chlorinated water or handling harsh chemicals. Clean gently using warm water, mild fragrance-free soap, and a soft-bristle brush. All Caen clients enjoy lifetime complimentary ultrasonic cleaning at our salons.
Our fine jewellery concierge is available for private high-definition video consultations, personal sizing advice, and bespoke diamond curation appointments Monday through Saturday.
Every creation arrives in our signature champagne presentation box wrapped with grosgrain ribbon, accompanied by an embossed wax-sealed certificate and an optional hand-calligraphed note card.
All shipments travel in anonymous, tamper-evident outer containers without brand insignia on the exterior to preserve total discretion and safety during transit. Direct recipient signature is mandatory on delivery.
